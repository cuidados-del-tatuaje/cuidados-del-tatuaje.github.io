## Thanks for trusting me with your tattoo! *Proper aftercare is essential for optimal healing and to keep the colours and details looking their best.* # IMPORTANT! During the Healing Process - Do not consume irritating foods (fried foods, greasy foods, spicy foods, etc.). - Do not consume alcohol or recreational drugs. - Do not scratch or pick at scabs or peeling skin. - 🔴 **Avoid swimming pools, spas/hot tubs, saunas, beaches, and prolonged baths for at least 3 weeks.** - Do not expose your tattoo to direct sunlight during the first 3 weeks 🟢 *(preferably for one month).* - Wear clean, loose-fitting clothing that does not cause excessive friction on the tattooed area. - Avoid activities that create excessive pressure or friction on the tattooed area. - Do not use products containing alcohol, fragrances, or exfoliants on the tattoo. - It is normal to experience mild redness, sensitivity, flaking, and moderate itching during the healing process. - If you notice signs such as severe pain, excessive swelling, persistent heat, or unusual discharge, please contact me via direct message. # Tattoo Aftercare Using Balm ## First Two Days - Keep the initial dressing on for the recommended time *(no longer than 24 hours).* - Wash your hands before touching the tattoo. - Remove the dressing and gently rinse the area with 🟢 **cool water** *(do not use soap).* - Allow it to air dry naturally *(do not use towels or other materials).* - Repeat this rinse in the morning and at night. ## From Day Three Onwards - Continue washing the tattoo in the morning and at night, then allow it to air dry naturally. - Once the tattoo is completely dry after washing, apply a very thin layer of tattoo balm and spread it evenly until it becomes completely transparent with no excess product remaining. - Repeat after each wash when the tattoo is dry *(twice daily).* - 🔴 **Avoid applying too much product.** The skin should remain moisturised, not saturated. # Tattoo Aftercare Using a Healing Patch ## First Few Days - Keep the healing patch on for **5 days**. - It is normal for fluid, ink, and plasma to build up underneath the patch. This is part of the healing process. - You may shower as usual, but avoid very hot water, prolonged showers, and direct water pressure on the patch. - Do not submerge the area in swimming pools, spas/hot tubs, the ocean, or bathtubs. ## Removing the Patch - Preferably remove it while showering or using warm water. - Peel it off slowly from one corner, stretching the patch parallel to the skin. - Wash the tattoo with a mild, fragrance-free soap and allow it to air dry naturally. ## After Removing the Patch - Apply a thin layer of **Lubriderm (blue cap)** moisturiser or tattoo balm. - Keep the skin moisturised without over-applying product. - Avoid scratching, rubbing, or picking at any scabs or peeling skin. ## Important - If the patch comes off completely before the recommended time, or if dirt or water gets underneath the edges, remove it, wash the area, and continue with the traditional aftercare routine using tattoo balm. # Once Healed - Apply **SPF 50+ sunscreen** whenever the tattoo is exposed to the sun to help preserve its vibrancy and overall quality. ## Note *Remember: a tattoo that is properly cared for during the first few months will look better for years to come. 🖤*
